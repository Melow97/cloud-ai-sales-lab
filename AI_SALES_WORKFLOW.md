# AI Solution Sales — End-to-End Opportunity Workflow

> **Independent illustrative portfolio exercise.** This is a process demonstration, not a record of a particular closed customer transaction. The author describes professional experience with AI product demonstrations and internal OpenAI-powered tools for workflow creation; product names, confidential architecture and client details are intentionally excluded.

## Eight-stage customer journey

| Stage | Customer-focused sales activities | Deliverable | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Prospect and qualify | Identify company priorities, decision makers, timeline, commercial opportunity | Opportunity brief | Business sponsor and next step identified |
| 2. Discover | Explore workflows, stakeholder needs, baseline KPIs, data, governance and budget | Discovery notes and stakeholder map | Clearly defined business problem |
| 3. Design workflow | Use approved internal AI-assisted tools to prototype a customer-relevant workflow; involve technical specialists for architecture | Demonstration plan and workflow diagram | Use case and feasibility agreed |
| 4. Demonstrate | Show the workflow from input to result, explain human review, limitations and implementation considerations | Demonstration and feedback summary | Customer agrees on evaluation criteria |
| 5. Build value case | Model benefits, implementation costs and TCO assumptions; identify risks | ROI/TCO hypothesis and proposal outline | Sponsor agrees on expected outcomes |
| 6. Pilot | Coordinate testing, data handling, technical validation and clear success measures | Proof-of-concept plan | Measured evidence supports decision |
| 7. Negotiate and close | Clarify commercial terms, commitments, buying approvals and rollout ownership | Decision and close plan | Contractual commitment |
| 8. Handoff and grow | Coordinate implementation stakeholders, adoption reviews and expansion opportunities | Customer success plan | Measurable utilization and outcomes |

## What the workflow demo should show

**Customer request** → **Discovery and requirements** → **AI-assisted workflow configuration** → **Demo or prototype** → **Human review and technical validation** → **Business case** → **Customer decision** → **Adoption follow-up**.

This demonstrates solution-selling orchestration, not model training or production AI engineering.
